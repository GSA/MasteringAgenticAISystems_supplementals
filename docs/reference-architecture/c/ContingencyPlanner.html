Contingency Planner

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:ContingencyPlanner

A task planner that, at planning time, generates a primary plan plus pre-computed conditional branches for the most likely, high-impact failure modes.

Responsibility. Produces branching plans with prepared responses for anticipated failures.

Also known as: Deliberative replanner, Conditional planner

Design guidance

  • SHOULD prepare a contingency only when failure probability x reactive delay exceeds the added planning overhead (P x D > C).
  • SHOULD limit contingencies to the few failure modes covering most disruptions (typically 2-5) and to one or two contingency levels, leaving rare compound failures to reactive replanning.
  • SHOULD derive failure frequencies from historical execution logs.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Branching plan took ~200 ms vs 50 ms for a linear plan but removed 2-3 s of replanning delay per empty-bin event (Ch5.6).
  • Planning for all combinations of N failure modes needs 2^N scenarios: 64 for 6, 1,024 for 10 (Ch5.6).
  • Three failure modes (60%, 20%, 10%) covered 90% of manufacturing disruptions (Ch5.6).
  • Delivery robot: 30% blockage probability x 4 s delay = 1.2 s expected vs 0.5 s contingency planning, 0.7 s net gain (Ch5.6).
