CPU Dataframe Engine

Software componentInfrastructureInfrastructurearc:CPUDataframeEngine

A dataframe compute engine that executes transformation operations on CPU cores.

Responsibility. Runs ETL transformations on general-purpose CPUs.

Also known as: CPU-based ETL

Variant of Dataframe Compute Engine abstract

When to choose. Choose for datasets under ~10 million documents, infrequent (monthly or rarer) processing, simple quality filters without deduplication, or limited capacity to manage GPU infrastructure.
