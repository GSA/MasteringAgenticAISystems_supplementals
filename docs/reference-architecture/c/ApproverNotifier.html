Approver Notifier

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:ApproverNotifier

A notification component that alerts assigned approvers of pending or escalated requests through channels they monitor, such as email, chat or mobile push.

Responsibility. Delivers approval request notifications with summary and dashboard link to approvers.

Also known as: Approval notification service, Approver alert badge, Stakeholder notification (simple escalation)
