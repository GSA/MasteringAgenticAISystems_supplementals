Regulatory Authority

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:RegulatoryAuthority

An external regulator or supervisory body that receives required documentation and transparency information and investigates whether an organization's AI systems comply with applicable law.

Responsibility. Receives breach notifications and reviews compliance records.

Also known as: Data protection authority, DPA, Supervisory authority, Regulator
