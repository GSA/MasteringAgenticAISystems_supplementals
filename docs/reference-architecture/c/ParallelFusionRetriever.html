Parallel Fusion Retriever

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:ParallelFusionRetriever

A hybrid retriever that queries the vector index and the knowledge graph independently in parallel and merges their results.

Responsibility. Queries vector and graph sources in parallel and merges results.

Also known as: Parallel querying, Hybrid vector-plus-graph RAG retrieval

Variant of Hybrid Retriever abstract

When to choose. Choose when you cannot predict whether vector search or the graph will surface the needed information, trading complexity for completeness.
