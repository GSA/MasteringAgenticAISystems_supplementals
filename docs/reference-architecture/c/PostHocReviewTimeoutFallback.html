Post-Hoc Review Timeout Fallback

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:PostHocReviewTimeoutFallback

A timeout fallback policy that automatically approves an expired, time-critical request while mandating retrospective human review that validates the decision after execution.

Responsibility. Unblocks time-critical decisions on approval timeout while preserving after-the-fact human validation.

Also known as: Auto-approve with mandatory post-hoc review

Variant of Approval Timeout Fallback Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose for time-critical decisions whose delay harms users (e.g., emergency claims or treatments) when approval queues exceed their SLA.
