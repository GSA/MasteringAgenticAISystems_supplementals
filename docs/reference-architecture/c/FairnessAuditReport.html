Fairness Audit Report

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:FairnessAuditReport

A documented record of per-group fairness metrics, detected disparities, their severity, and the methodology used for an audit cycle.

Responsibility. Records the results of a fairness audit for accountability and disclosure.

Also known as: Bias audit report, Disparate impact report, Published fairness metrics
