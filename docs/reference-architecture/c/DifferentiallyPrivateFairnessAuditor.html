Differentially Private Fairness Auditor

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:DifferentiallyPrivateFairnessAuditor

A privacy-preserving fairness auditor that adds calibrated noise to demographic counts and fairness metrics under a tunable privacy budget (epsilon).

Responsibility. Publishes noisy aggregate fairness metrics with differential privacy guarantees.

Variant of Privacy-Preserving Fairness Auditor abstract

When to choose. Choose when fairness metrics must be released with provable guarantees that no individual's participation or outcome can be inferred, and sample sizes are large enough to tolerate added noise.
