Operational Runbook

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:OperationalRunbook

A documented diagnosis-and-mitigation procedure for a recurring operational issue, such as high latency or high error rate, that branches by bottleneck or error type to specific checks and actions.

Responsibility. Prescribes diagnosis steps and mitigations for a known issue type.

Also known as: Runbook, Documented procedures, AI incident response procedure, Incident response plan

Design guidance

  • SHOULD maintain runbooks for common issues that start with a short diagnosis (about 2 minutes) identifying the bottleneck step or error type.
  • SHOULD specify who is notified, who investigates, immediate actions, affected-party communication and recovery steps.
  • SHOULD document the incident response plan and exercise it regularly with the on-call team.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • High-latency runbook triggers at > 10 s response latency; high-error-rate runbook at > 2% error rate (Ref8.06).
