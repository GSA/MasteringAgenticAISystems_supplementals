Proactive Explanation Expander

Software componentExperienceExperience & Human Oversightarc:ProactiveExplanationExpander

An expansion controller that automatically opens detailed explanation layers, or shows guided hints, when confidence is low, outcomes are unexpected, decisions deviate from history, or stakes are high.

Responsibility. Automatically surfaces detailed explanations when risk signals warrant them.

Also known as: Intelligent expansion, Guided disclosure, Automatic escalation of explanation depth, Adaptive disclosure

Variant of Explanation Expansion Controller abstract

When to choose. Choose when confidence may fall below thresholds, decisions may deviate from historical patterns, or high-stakes scenarios arise, so critical detail must surface without user initiative.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD automatically expand when confidence falls below established thresholds, decisions deviate from historical patterns, or high-stakes scenarios emerge.
  • SHOULD provide contextual hints stating what deeper layers contain and when they might change the user's assessment.
  • High-confidence straightforward cases SHOULD receive minimal explanation (e.g., a simple green indicator).
