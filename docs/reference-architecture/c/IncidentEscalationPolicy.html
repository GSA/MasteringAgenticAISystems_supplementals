Incident Escalation Policy

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:IncidentEscalationPolicy

A configuration defining the ordered responders and time thresholds through which an unresolved production incident escalates.

Responsibility. Specifies who is escalated to and when for unresolved incidents.

Also known as: Escalation ladder, Incident response plan, Incident severity classification

Relationships

configures structural

  • Incident Manager Ch8.2A Ch9.4 +4

Design guidance

  • SHOULD designate breach-response personnel, communication protocols for individuals and authorities, and forensic procedures, tested through simulated breach exercises.
  • SHOULD map severity to response: CRITICAL pages the incident commander and engages executives and legal; HIGH pages the response team and notifies the product owner; MEDIUM opens a ticket; LOW is logged for the next review.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • 30/60/90-minute escalation thresholds (Ref7.16).
  • Critical burn-rate alerts page on-call immediately (even at 3 AM) and escalate to leadership (Ch8.2A).
