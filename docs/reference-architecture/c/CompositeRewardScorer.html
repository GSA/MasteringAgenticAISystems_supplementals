Composite Reward Scorer

Software componentModel AdaptationModelsarc:CompositeRewardScorer

A reward scorer, often itself an agent, that synthesizes human-preference scores with factuality verification, instruction-following metrics and safety-filter signals into one reward.

Responsibility. Grounds preference rewards with objective correctness and safety signals.

Also known as: Agentic reward modeling, Reward agent, Hybrid reward function

Variant of Reward Scorer abstract

When to choose. Choose when human preference must be grounded by verifiable correctness, e.g., to prevent optimizing toward fluent hallucinations or annotator bias.
