Binary Rating Feedback Prompt

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:BinaryRatingFeedbackPrompt

A feedback collector that asks for a single-click thumbs-up/down or star rating immediately after an agent response.

Responsibility. Captures high-volume, low-effort explicit satisfaction signals.

Also known as: Thumbs up/down, Was this helpful?

Variant of Feedback Collector abstract

When to choose. Choose when high feedback volume matters more than information density; single-click ratings generate high volume but low information per response.
