Deferred Feedback Survey

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:DeferredFeedbackSurvey

A feedback collector that requests feedback after the interaction, such as emailed requests hours later or periodic satisfaction surveys.

Responsibility. Gathers retrospective, relationship-level satisfaction and feature feedback outside the conversation.

Also known as: Regular surveys, Email feedback request, Monthly survey

Variant of Feedback Collector abstract

When to choose. Choose when periodic overall satisfaction, frequency and feature-request data are needed (Ref10.03), accepting lower response rates as context fades.
