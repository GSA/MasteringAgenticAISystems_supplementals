Experience · Human role

End User

Human roleExperienceExperience & Human Oversightarc:EndUser

A human principal who submits requests to an agent, reviews its outputs and explanations, and can intervene in or undo its actions.

Responsibility. Directs and reviews agent work through the user interface.

Also known as: User, Overseer, Decision-reviewer, Compliance Officer (as questioner), Customer, Loan applicant, Affected party
