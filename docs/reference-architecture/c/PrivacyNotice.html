Privacy Notice

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:PrivacyNotice

A plain-language disclosure telling individuals what personal data is collected, for which purposes, who can access it, how long it is retained and which rights they hold.

Responsibility. Informs data subjects about processing so consent and rights are meaningful.

Also known as: Privacy policy, Data transparency disclosure, Consent explanation

Design guidance

  • SHOULD use simple language rather than legal speak and be easy to find.
  • SHOULD disclose AI involvement, its purpose and known limitations where AI is used (Ref9.03).
