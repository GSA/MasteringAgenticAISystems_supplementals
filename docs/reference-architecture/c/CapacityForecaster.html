Capacity Forecaster

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:CapacityForecaster

An analysis component that projects future GPU, memory, bandwidth and request-rate needs from historical utilization and growth trends to plan capacity ahead of demand.

Responsibility. Forecasts future capacity requirements from historical usage trends.

Also known as: Capacity planning, Cost trend extrapolation, Projected monthly cost

Design guidance

  • SHOULD plan capacity ahead of forecast and account for seasonal variation.
  • SHOULD extrapolate cost trends to trigger capacity-planning decisions (optimize, raise budget, raise prices or limit feature access) before budgets are exceeded.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Analyze 3 months of history, project a 6-month forecast and plan capacity 30% ahead of forecast (Ref7.16).
