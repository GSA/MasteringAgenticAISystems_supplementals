Regional Constitution

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:RegionalConstitution

A constitution composed of universal core principles plus culturally-adapted, jurisdiction-specific principles that respect local legal requirements without contradicting core safety commitments.

Responsibility. Specifies region-specific principles layered on universal core principles.

Also known as: Core plus culturally-adapted principles, Regional constitution variant, Geographically gated principles

Variant of Constitution abstract

When to choose. Choose for globally deployed systems whose jurisdictions differ in law and cultural norms (e.g., political speech, religious expression), accepting higher design, evaluation and maintenance cost.
