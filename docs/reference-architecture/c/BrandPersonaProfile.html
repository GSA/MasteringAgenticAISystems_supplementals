Brand Persona Profile

Data artifactExperienceExperience & Human Oversightarc:BrandPersonaProfile

A configuration of a conversational agent's tone, personality and communication norms aligned with organizational brand voice and audience expectations.

Responsibility. Specifies the conversational voice the agent uses toward users.

Also known as: Bot persona, Brand voice configuration

Relationships

configures structural

  • LLM Inference Service Ch10.1

Design guidance

  • SHOULD match conversational tone to organizational voice (professional and precise for financial institutions, chatty and playful for consumer brands).
  • MUST NOT use persona or anthropomorphism to conceal system limitations or present fabricated facts as certain.
