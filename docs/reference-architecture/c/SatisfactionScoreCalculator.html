Satisfaction Score Calculator

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:SatisfactionScoreCalculator

An analytics component that aggregates post-interaction survey responses into standard satisfaction indices: top-2-box Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT), Net Promoter Score (NPS) and mean Customer Effort Score (CES).

Responsibility. Computes CSAT, NPS and CES from stored survey responses.

Also known as: CSAT calculator, NPS calculator, CES calculator, User satisfaction metrics

Design guidance

  • MUST compute CSAT as the share of ratings 4 or 5 (top-2-box) among all responses.
  • MUST compute NPS as percent promoters (9-10) minus percent detractors (0-6), ignoring passives (7-8).
  • SHOULD interpret CES inversely (lower is better); for routine interactions it often predicts retention better than CSAT.
  • SHOULD interpret the three indices together, since each alone can mislead.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Worked example (1,000 responses): CSAT 850/1,000 = 85%; NPS 45% - 15% = +30 with 40% passives; CES 2.8 (Ch8.4).
  • CSAT benchmarks: 70% acceptable for chatbots, 75% good, 80%+ excellent; NPS: +20 acceptable, +40 good, +60+ excellent; CES 2.8 comfortably beats a typical 3.5 target (Ch8.4).
