Satisfaction Survey Definition

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:SatisfactionSurveyDefinition

A versioned post-interaction questionnaire specifying the satisfaction (1-5), likelihood-to-recommend (0-10) and customer-effort (1-7) questions and scales used to measure agent user experience.

Responsibility. Defines the survey questions and response scales from which satisfaction indices are computed.

Also known as: CSAT/NPS/CES survey, Post-interaction survey questions

Design guidance

  • SHOULD ask three distinct questions: satisfaction with the interaction (1-5), likelihood to recommend (0-10) and effort required to resolve the issue (1-7, 1 = very low effort).
