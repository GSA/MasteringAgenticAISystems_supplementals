Risk Monitor

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:RiskMonitor

A governance component that periodically re-estimates each registered risk's probability and impact from live signals such as drift and vulnerability scans, updates the register and escalates threshold crossings.

Responsibility. Keeps registered risk scores current and escalates risks that exceed their thresholds.

Also known as: Continuous risk monitoring
