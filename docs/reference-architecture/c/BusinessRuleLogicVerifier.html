Business Rule Logic Verifier

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:BusinessRuleLogicVerifier

A reasoning verifier that checks whether agent conclusions satisfy business rules and domain constraints encoded as mathematical logic.

Responsibility. Verifies agent conclusions against formally encoded business constraints.

Also known as: Automated reasoning checks, Constraint validator

Variant of Reasoning Verifier abstract

When to choose. Choose in regulated industries where business rules and domain constraints can be encoded as formal logic and compliance must be verifiably met.
