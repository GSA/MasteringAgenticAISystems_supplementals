Decision Stakeholder

Human roleHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:DecisionStakeholder

A human principal (e.g., patient and physician, vehicle owner, fleet operator, regulator, ethicist) who chooses a preferred trade-off and supplies preference and risk parameters.

Responsibility. Selects the preferred trade-off among presented alternatives according to their own values.

Also known as: Principal, Policymaker, Shared decision-maker, Affected individual, Applicant, Data subject, Affected community representative, Civil rights organization, Patient, Regulator, Affected community, Job applicant
