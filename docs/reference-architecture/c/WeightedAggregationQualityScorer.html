Weighted Aggregation Quality Scorer

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:WeightedAggregationQualityScorer

A reasoning quality scorer that computes a weighted average of dimension scores with weights reflecting application priorities.

Responsibility. Scores reasoning quality by application-weighted dimension importance.

Variant of Reasoning Quality Scorer abstract

When to choose. Choose when dimensions carry different importance, e.g., high-stakes medical reasoning weighting correctness over efficiency or consumer chatbots weighting relevancy and informativeness.
