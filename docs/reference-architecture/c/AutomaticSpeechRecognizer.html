Automatic Speech Recognizer

Software componentExperienceExperience & Human OversightVariation point (abstract)arc:AutomaticSpeechRecognizer

An abstract speech-to-text service that converts spoken audio into text transcripts for consumption by an agent or downstream processing.

Responsibility. Transcribes user or recorded speech into text.

Also known as: ASR service, Speech-to-text service, Voice input modality handler, Voice input

Variants

VariantWhen to choose
Speech TranscriberChoose for post-hoc analysis (call-center QA, meeting summaries, voicemail), maximum accuracy (legal, medical), speaker diarization or overlapping multi-speaker audio, where higher latency is acceptable.
Streaming Speech RecognizerChoose when real-time interaction is required (live customer-service calls, voice assistants, live accessibility captioning) and latency matters more than perfect accuracy.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD bias decoding toward domain terminology via word boosting or a custom language model when domain terms are acoustically ambiguous.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Custom language models cut domain-terminology errors by 30-50% and overall WER by 5-10%; word boosting improves domain-term accuracy by 5-15% when acoustic evidence is ambiguous (Ch7.5).
