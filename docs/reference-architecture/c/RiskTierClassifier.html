AI Risk Tier Classifier

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:RiskTierClassifier

A governance component that assigns each inventoried AI system a risk category from failure impact, affected populations, applicable regulation and organizational dependence, so controls can be proportionate.

Responsibility. Categorizes inventoried AI systems into risk tiers for proportional governance.

Also known as: Risk categorization, Risk tiering
