Profiling Tier Policy

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:ProfilingTierPolicy

A policy defining tiered profiling modes: continuous lightweight monitoring, triggered detailed captures, canary profiling of a traffic fraction, and restricted exhaustive kernel profiling.

Responsibility. Balances profiling overhead against diagnostic value across modes.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD run continuous low-overhead profiling 24/7 and trigger detailed captures only for specific diagnoses.
  • MAY instrument a small percentage of production traffic (canary profiling) for real-world visibility.

As stated by the sources; verify before use.
