Data Quality Gate

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:DataQualityGate

An enforcement component that applies accept, flag or reject policy to validation results per document and per batch, keeping enforcement separate from validation logic.

Responsibility. Decides whether validated data may proceed into the knowledge base.

Also known as: Enforcement policy, Batch rejection gate

Design guidance

  • SHOULD reject whole batches that exceed the completeness threshold, forcing source-system remediation.
  • SHOULD accept warning-level documents while flagging them for cleanup to balance quality with coverage.
