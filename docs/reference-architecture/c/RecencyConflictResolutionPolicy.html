Recency Conflict Resolution Policy

Data artifactCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:RecencyConflictResolutionPolicy

A conflict resolution policy that fires the most recently added or modified matching rule first.

Responsibility. Resolves rule conflicts by preferring newer rules.

Variant of Conflict Resolution Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose when knowledge evolves rapidly and newer rules encode more current understanding (e.g., new fraud patterns); requires rule modification timestamps and clear documentation.
