Content Freshness Monitor

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:ContentFreshnessMonitor

A monitoring component that flags knowledge base documents not updated within their expected refresh interval so they are not used as grounding.

Responsibility. Detects stale documents in retrieval sources.

Also known as: Staleness monitoring, Staleness alerting, Knowledge drift detection, Knowledge Staleness Detector, Temporal validation, Staleness detection, Content age monitoring

Design guidance

  • SHOULD flag documents exceeding expected refresh intervals before agents retrieve them.
  • SHOULD trigger a review process deciding whether aged documents remain valid or need updating, especially in time-sensitive domains.
  • SHOULD trigger review workflows for documents older than defined thresholds; it catches staleness but not content that was wrong when published.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Alert if articles are not updated for 7+ days, prices are older than 1 day, weather data older than 2 hours, or stock prices delayed > 5 minutes (Ref8.04).
