Parametric Answer Generator

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:ParametricAnswerGenerator

An answer-generation component that prompts the LLM to answer from its parametric (weight-encoded) knowledge without retrieved context, used for retrieval-free queries and as a retrieval fallback.

Responsibility. Answers queries without retrieval from the model's parametric knowledge.

Also known as: Direct parametric generation, No-retrieval generation
