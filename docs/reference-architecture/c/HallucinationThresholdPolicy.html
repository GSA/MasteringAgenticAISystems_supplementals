Hallucination Threshold Policy

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:HallucinationThresholdPolicy

A policy artifact setting maximum acceptable hallucination rates per severity level and application domain for deployment and alerting decisions.

Responsibility. Declares domain- and severity-specific hallucination tolerances.

Also known as: Domain-specific hallucination thresholds, Severity thresholds

Design guidance

  • MUST set thresholds by failure consequence per domain and severity rather than one aggregate target.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Severity thresholds: critical <0.1-0.5%, high <1-2%, medium <5%, low <10-15% (Ch3.10).
  • Domain thresholds: financial advice <2%, customer support <5%, healthcare critical <0.5%, entertainment <20% (Ch3.10).
  • Production baseline hallucination rates range 8-20%; customer-facing targets <5% (Ch3.10).
  • Alert when hallucination rate exceeds 2% (Ref8.03).
