Content Deny List

Data artifactSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:ContentDenyList

A maintained list of forbidden words, phrases, regular expressions and known harmful patterns, including domain-specific compliance phrases, drawn from static threat intelligence and dynamic security-team updates.

Responsibility. Enumerates the patterns a deny-list filter must block.

Also known as: Deny list, Blocklist, Profanity list, Injection pattern list
