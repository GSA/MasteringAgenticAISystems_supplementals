DAgger Trainer

Software componentModel AdaptationModelsarc:DAggerTrainer

An imitation learner that iteratively executes its current policy, has an expert label the visited states, aggregates these labels into the dataset, and retrains.

Responsibility. Aggregates expert labels for policy-visited states and retrains iteratively.

Also known as: Dataset Aggregation, DAgger

Variant of Imitation Learner abstract

When to choose. Choose when experts can provide repeated labels and the learned policy can be safely executed during training to expose its failure modes.
