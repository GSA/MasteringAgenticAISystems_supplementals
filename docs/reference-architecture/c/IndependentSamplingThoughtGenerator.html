Independent Sampling Thought Generator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:IndependentSamplingThoughtGenerator

A thought generator that samples each of k candidates independently from the current state without conditioning on previously generated candidates.

Responsibility. Samples mutually independent candidate thoughts to maximize diversity.

Also known as: i.i.d. thought sampling, Parallel brainstorming

Variant of Thought Generator abstract

When to choose. Choose for open-ended exploration of vast, unstructured solution spaces where large conceptual leaps are needed (e.g., creative writing plans).
