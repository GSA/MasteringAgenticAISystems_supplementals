DPIA Record

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:DPIARecord

A living Data Protection Impact Assessment recording identified risks with harm pathways, data-flow mapping, mitigations, stakeholder impact, necessity and proportionality justification, residual risk and an approval decision.

Responsibility. Documents and governs high-risk personal-data processing risks before and during deployment.

Also known as: Data Protection Impact Assessment, Privacy impact assessment

Design guidance

  • MUST be completed before large-scale processing, automated decisions with legal or significant effects, sensitive-category processing, novel technology or consequential AI/ML decisions.
  • MUST be revised when new data sources are added or model capabilities expand.
  • SHOULD be reviewed by others and conclude proceed, proceed with mitigation, or redesign (Ref9.05).
  • SHOULD test whether purposes could be achieved with less data, e.g., pseudonymized claim IDs instead of names.
