Weighted Round-Robin Load Balancer

Software componentInfrastructureInfrastructurearc:WeightedRoundRobinLoadBalancer

A load balancer that distributes requests in proportion to per-replica weights reflecting capacity, cost or rollout stage.

Responsibility. Routes traffic proportionally to configured replica weights.

Also known as: Weighted distribution, Capacity-aware routing

Variant of Load Balancer abstract

When to choose. Choose when replicas run on heterogeneous infrastructure with measurable capacity differences, or for weighted gradual rollouts.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD assign weights automatically (default or metadata-based) to replicas launched by autoscaling.
  • SHOULD monitor actual traffic share against configured weights to detect misconfiguration.
  • MAY be combined with least-connections routing.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Weights 4:1:1 send 67% of traffic to a GPU replica and 17% to each CPU replica (Ch4.7).
  • Document case: GPU replicas 3x faster; weight 3 vs 1 sent 60% to two GPU replicas and ~13% to each of three CPU replicas; with least connections, P95 26 s and 85% average GPU utilization (Ch4.7).
