Spend Budget Enforcer

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:SpendBudgetEnforcer

A control component that compares a principal's accumulated cost with its spending budget, warning at a soft threshold and rejecting further requests at the hard limit.

Responsibility. Blocks or flags requests from principals whose spend reaches budget thresholds.

Also known as: Budget enforcement, enforce_budget

Design guidance

  • SHOULD reject requests from a customer whose cost reaches the hard budget limit and alert operators.
  • MAY be combined with rate limiting and more conservative capacity plans to contain budget overruns.
