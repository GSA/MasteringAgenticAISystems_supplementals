Output Risk Stratifier

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:OutputRiskStratifier

A routing component that classifies each output by criticality and applies a correspondingly lighter or stricter chain of output controls.

Responsibility. Selects output scrutiny level by output criticality.

Also known as: Risk-stratified output controls, Stakes-based display strategy, Decision stakes assessment

Design guidance

  • SHOULD apply lighter filtering to general information and stricter scrutiny (fact checking, human approval) to high-criticality outputs such as medication changes.
  • Stakes assessment SHOULD be context-sensitive: a normally low-stakes recommendation becomes high-stakes if it may expose children to harm or spread health misinformation.
