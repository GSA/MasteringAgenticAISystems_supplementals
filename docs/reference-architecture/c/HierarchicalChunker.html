Hierarchical Chunker

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:HierarchicalChunker

A document chunker that produces nested chunks mirroring document structure (document, section, paragraph) so each chunk retains its structural context.

Responsibility. Splits documents into structure-preserving nested chunks.

Also known as: Hierarchical chunking, Recursive chunking

Variant of Document Chunker abstract

When to choose. Choose when document structure (sections, subsections) must be preserved so retrieved chunks keep their structural context, accepting more complex retrieval logic.
