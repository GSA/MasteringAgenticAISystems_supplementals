Circuit Breaker Policy

Data artifactTools & IntegrationOrchestration & Toolsarc:CircuitBreakerPolicy

A per-dependency configuration of failure-count or failure-rate thresholds, measurement window, recovery timeout, and half-open trial requests for a circuit breaker.

Responsibility. Sets the trip and recovery thresholds of a circuit breaker for one dependency.

Also known as: Circuit breaker thresholds

Design guidance

  • SHOULD use more aggressive thresholds for less reliable dependencies such as external APIs and more lenient ones for more reliable LLM APIs.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.
