Compliance Officer

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ComplianceOfficer

A human role accountable for verifying that evaluation protocols, approved model versions and reviewed prompts satisfy regulatory requirements before production promotion.

Responsibility. Signs off regulatory conformance of agent releases.

Also known as: Compliance team, Rule base auditor, Compliance auditor, Legal counsel reviewer

Design guidance

  • SHOULD be formally designated with a governance committee, documented requirements and regular audits (Ref9.03).
  • SHOULD be appointed and receive regulatory and critical-audit alerts directly.
  • SHOULD verify through testing and monitoring that technical implementations achieve compliance objectives, in collaboration with AI engineers and leadership, rather than relying on legal review alone.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Quarterly compliance reviews of data-handling changes and annual comprehensive audits (Ch9.7).
