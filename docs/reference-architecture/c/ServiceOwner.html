Service Owner

Human roleHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:ServiceOwner

An accountable owner of a production agent service who receives incidents that on-call engineers and team leads have not resolved.

Responsibility. Owns final resolution of escalated production incidents for a service.

Also known as: Service owner, Leadership escalation, Accountable human decision-maker, Model owner, System owner, Finding owner, System Owner, Product owner, Customer service manager
