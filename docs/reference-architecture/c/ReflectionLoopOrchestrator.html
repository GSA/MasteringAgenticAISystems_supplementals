Reflection Loop Orchestrator

Software componentOrchestrationOrchestration & Toolsarc:ReflectionLoopOrchestrator

A coordinator that manages the generate-critique-refine cycle between producer and reflection agents and decides when output quality is sufficient to stop.

Responsibility. Coordinates reflection cycles and their termination.

Also known as: Master orchestrator (multi-agent reflection loop), Generate-verify-revise loop
