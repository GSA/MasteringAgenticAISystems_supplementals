Formal Proof Verifier

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:FormalProofVerifier

A reasoning verifier that translates agent reasoning into formal logical statements and checks them with a proof system, either proving correctness or pinpointing the violating step.

Responsibility. Proves or refutes reasoning steps using formal logic.

Also known as: Formal verification, Theorem-prover verification

Variant of Reasoning Verifier abstract

When to choose. Choose for mathematical, symbolic or other formally specified domains where errors have severe consequences (financial calculation, cryptographic protocols, safety-critical systems) and reasoning can be translated into formal statements.
