Change Manager

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ChangeManager

A designated human who leads the phased organizational rollout of an agent system: preparation, announcement, training, pilot, staged rollout and stabilization, including addressing resistance.

Responsibility. Leads organizational change management for agent adoption.

Also known as: Change team lead

Design guidance

  • SHOULD pilot with a small subset of users under close monitoring before staged rollout.
  • SHOULD address resistance types (understanding, trust, job threat, complexity) with targeted actions.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Six phases over ~26 weeks; staged rollout waves of 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% of users at weeks 15, 17, 19, 21 (Ref10.04).
