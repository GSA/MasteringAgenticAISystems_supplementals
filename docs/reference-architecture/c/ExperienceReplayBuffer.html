Experience Replay Buffer

Data storeMemoryCognition & Memoryarc:ExperienceReplayBuffer

A store of past experience transitions (state, action, reward, next state) from which batches are sampled to retrain learned models, mixing old and new experience.

Responsibility. Retains past experience transitions for repeated sampling during training.

Also known as: Replay buffer, Episodic replay memory, Replay memory
