Telemetry Retention Policy

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:TelemetryRetentionPolicy

A policy stating how long metrics, logs, traces and cost data are retained in hot and archive tiers and at what resolution.

Responsibility. Bounds retention duration and resolution of operational telemetry.

Also known as: Observability data retention

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Metrics 3 months at 1-minute resolution; logs 2 weeks hot/30 days archive; traces 1 week hot/30 days archive; cost data 2 years (Ref7.16).
