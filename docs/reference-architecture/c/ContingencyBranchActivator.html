Contingency Branch Activator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:ContingencyBranchActivator

A replanner that, when an observed condition matches a prepared failure signature, immediately switches execution to the corresponding pre-computed contingency branch.

Responsibility. Activates the matching pre-computed contingency branch without search at execution time.

Also known as: Tactical contingency layer, Layer 2 contingency activation

Variant of Replanner abstract

When to choose. Choose when failures are frequent (>30%), predictable from observable conditions (time of day, sensor state), stable, few (2-5 modes), and replanning pauses of 3-5 s are intolerable.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD be used for failures that are frequent, predictable from observable conditions, and stable over time.
  • MUST hand novel failures (no matching contingency) to search-based replanning.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Choose contingency planning when >30% of executions hit anticipated scenarios; at a 5% failure rate it cost 43 s/day vs 25 s reactive (Ch5.6).
  • Tactical layer operates on a 1-10 second timescale (Ch5.6).
