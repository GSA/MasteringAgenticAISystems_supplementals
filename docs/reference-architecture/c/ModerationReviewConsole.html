Moderation Review Console

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:ModerationReviewConsole

A review interface presenting a queued content item with the original query, proposed response, multi-classifier scores and highlighted concerning passages, and capturing the moderator's decision and justification.

Responsibility. Presents queued content for moderator decision and records the outcome.

Also known as: Borderline case moderator interface
