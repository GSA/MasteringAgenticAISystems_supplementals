Minimum Aggregation Quality Scorer

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:MinimumAggregationQualityScorer

A reasoning quality scorer that takes the minimum of the dimension scores, treating a chain as only as strong as its weakest dimension.

Responsibility. Scores reasoning quality by its bottleneck dimension.

Variant of Reasoning Quality Scorer abstract

When to choose. Choose when agents must not receive full credit for correct answers reached through flawed reasoning and the bottleneck dimension should be surfaced.
