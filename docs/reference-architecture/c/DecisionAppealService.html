Decision Appeal Service

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:DecisionAppealService

A recourse component through which individuals affected by an automated decision challenge it, submitting the case for human review that can overturn the decision.

Responsibility. Accepts appeals of automated decisions and routes them to human reviewers.

Also known as: Appeal process, Recourse mechanism, User complaint intake, Appeal procedure, Contest mechanism, Decision Appeal Handler, Appeal trigger
