Reasoning Strategy Refiner

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:ReasoningStrategyRefiner

A metacognitive component that analyses failed reasoning graphs post-execution for failure modes (premature aggregation, unproductive refinement loops, erroneous pruning) and revises reasoning-strategy templates.

Responsibility. Revises reasoning-strategy templates from failures of past reasoning graphs.

Also known as: GoO refiner, Metacognitive refinement
