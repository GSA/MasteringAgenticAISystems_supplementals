Fuzzy Text Deduplicator

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:FuzzyTextDeduplicator

A deduplicator that compares documents with a length-normalized string distance and removes those whose similarity exceeds a configured threshold.

Responsibility. Removes near-duplicate documents with minor textual differences.

Also known as: Fuzzy matching, Near-duplicate detection

Variant of Content Deduplicator abstract

When to choose. Choose when near-duplicates differ by minor edits, dates or formatting (versioning, format conversion); tune the threshold between conservative and aggressive.
