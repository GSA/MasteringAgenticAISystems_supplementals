Spend Budget

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:SpendBudget

A declared spending limit for a customer, feature or environment over a period, with warning and hard-limit utilization thresholds.

Responsibility. States how much may be spent and at which utilization levels to warn or block.

Also known as: Budget, Monthly customer budget, Cost targets

Design guidance

  • SHOULD define both a warning threshold and a hard-limit threshold.
  • SHOULD be compared with actual spend on organization-level dashboards (budget versus actual).

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Warn at 80% and hard-limit (reject requests) at 95% of the monthly budget (Ref8.05).
  • Cost targets: development < $100/day, staging < $200/day, small production < $500/day, large production < $5,000/day (Ref8.09).
