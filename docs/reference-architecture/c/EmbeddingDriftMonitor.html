Embedding Drift Monitor

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:EmbeddingDriftMonitor

A monitoring component that tracks distribution statistics of generated embeddings, such as mean vector norm and per-dimension variance, and flags sudden changes.

Responsibility. Detects embedding model or input-encoding faults before invalid vectors populate the knowledge base.

Also known as: Embedding quality metrics tracking
