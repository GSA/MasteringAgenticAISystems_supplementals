Gradual Rollback Controller

Software componentInfrastructureInfrastructurearc:GradualRollbackController

A rollback controller that reverses a progressive rollout stepwise, reducing the new version's traffic share and checking stability at each step.

Responsibility. Performs a staged traffic reversion to the previous version.

Also known as: Controlled rollback

Variant of Version Rollback Controller abstract

When to choose. Choose for moderate quality degradation, performance issues or non-critical bugs.
