Iterative Refinement Coordinator

Software componentOrchestrationOrchestration & Toolsarc:IterativeRefinementCoordinator

A coordinator that runs repeated bidirectional exchange cycles between neural and symbolic components, feeding symbolic hypotheses back as attention cues, until confidence, diminishing-returns, or iteration-limit criteria are met.

Responsibility. Coordinates neural-symbolic feedback cycles until convergence or a stop criterion.

Also known as: Cooperative architecture, Iterative pattern controller
