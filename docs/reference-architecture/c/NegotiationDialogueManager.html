Negotiation Dialogue Manager

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:NegotiationDialogueManager

A cognition component that exchanges proposals, preferences, constraints and reasoning with a human counterpart and recomputes proposals under revised constraint sets until a mutually acceptable solution emerges.

Responsibility. Conducts turn-based negotiation with a human toward a compromise solution.

Also known as: Negotiation protocol, Collaborative problem-solving agent
