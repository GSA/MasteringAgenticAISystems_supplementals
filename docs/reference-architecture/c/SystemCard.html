System Card

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:SystemCard

A system-level document extending model cards to a deployed AI system, describing how component models, datasets, guardrails and humans interact, including conflict handling, degradation behavior and the human-AI decision model.

Responsibility. Documents emergent behavior and interactions of a multi-component AI system.
