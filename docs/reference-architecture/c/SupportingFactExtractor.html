Supporting Fact Extractor

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:SupportingFactExtractor

A component that isolates the specific sentences within retrieved passages that support an answer, rather than passing whole passages to reasoning.

Responsibility. Identifies sentence-level supporting facts in retrieved passages.

Also known as: Fact-level extraction, Supporting fact identification
