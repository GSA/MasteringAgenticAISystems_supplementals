Precedent Case Retriever

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:PrecedentCaseRetriever

A retrieval component that finds the most similar previously decided cases, with their final outcomes and reviewer reasoning, using similarity over case characteristics and demographics, to contextualize a current decision.

Responsibility. Retrieves comparable historical cases and their outcomes for a current decision.

Also known as: Historical comparison panel, Precedent case display, Similar case retrieval
