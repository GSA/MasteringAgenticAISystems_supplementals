Risk Acceptance Record

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:RiskAcceptanceRecord

A signed record formally accepting a residual AI risk, stating business justification, monitoring plan, operating conditions, approvers and a re-assessment review date.

Responsibility. Documents the conscious, conditional acceptance of a residual risk.

Also known as: Risk acceptance form
