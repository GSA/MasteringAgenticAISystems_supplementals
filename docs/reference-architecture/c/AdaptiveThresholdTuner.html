Adaptive Threshold Tuner

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:AdaptiveThresholdTuner

A feedback component that analyses human approval, modification, and rejection rates and adjusts escalation thresholds to observed user or team risk tolerance.

Responsibility. Tunes escalation thresholds from approval outcomes.

Also known as: Smart thresholds, Approval-rate tracker, Rejection reason analyzer

Design guidance

  • SHOULD track approval rates and relax thresholds when reviewers approve nearly everything, tightening them when rejections are frequent.
  • SHOULD adjust confidence thresholds and policy parameters from shadow-period comparisons of human judgments with agent recommendations before activating enforcement.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Approving >80% of requests without modification suggests the threshold is too low (Ch1.1A).
  • If users approve 95% of refunds under $1,000, raise auto-approval to $1,200 then $1,500; if 40% of flagged contracts are rejected, lower the escalation threshold (Ch1.1B).
