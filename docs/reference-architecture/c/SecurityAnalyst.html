Safety & Security · Human role

Security Analyst

Human roleSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:SecurityAnalyst

A security or ML-safety team member who investigates safety-violation alerts, adversarial prompting patterns and the effectiveness of guardrail policies.

Responsibility. Responds to safety-violation spikes by hardening prompts and guardrail policies.

Also known as: Security team, Security/ML team, Security operations center staff
