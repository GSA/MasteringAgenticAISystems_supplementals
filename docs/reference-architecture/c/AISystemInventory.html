Design guidance

  • MUST catalogue all AI systems across business units before selecting controls; inventories routinely reveal more systems than expected.
  • SHOULD record for each system what it does, who uses it and who is affected by its decisions.
  • SHOULD track lifecycle status from planning through retirement so governance continues until decommissioning.
