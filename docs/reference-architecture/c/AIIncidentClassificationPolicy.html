AI Incident Classification Policy

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:AIIncidentClassificationPolicy

A policy defining what constitutes an AI incident (performance degradation beyond thresholds, bias exceeding acceptable levels, security compromise, harmful outputs reaching users, regulatory violation) and its severity classes.

Responsibility. Determines which AI events are incidents and how severe they are.

Also known as: Incident criteria, Incident classification

Design guidance
