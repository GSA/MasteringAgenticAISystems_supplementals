Preference Reward Scorer

Software componentModel AdaptationModelsarc:PreferenceRewardScorer

A reward scorer whose reward is solely the learned reward model's prediction of human preference.

Responsibility. Rewards responses by predicted human preference alone.

Also known as: Traditional RLHF reward

Variant of Reward Scorer abstract

When to choose. Choose when desired behaviour is a subjective judgment (e.g., tone, trade-offs) that rigid rules cannot capture and annotator bias is controlled.
