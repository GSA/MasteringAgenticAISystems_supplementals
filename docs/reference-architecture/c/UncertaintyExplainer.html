Uncertainty Explainer

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:UncertaintyExplainer

An explanation component that itemizes the sources of uncertainty behind a confidence level and states which additional information or investigation would raise confidence.

Responsibility. Explains why a decision's confidence is limited and what would increase it.

Also known as: Interactive confidence exploration, Detailed uncertainty analysis
