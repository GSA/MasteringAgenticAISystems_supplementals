Intervention Timing Optimizer

Software componentExperienceExperience & Human Oversightarc:InterventionTimingOptimizer

A scheduling component that chooses when to deliver a proactive intervention by weighing the user's current engagement and availability, habitual receptivity windows, and external urgency or opportunity windows.

Responsibility. Selects the delivery moment for each proactive intervention.

Also known as: Temporal intelligence, Notification timing engine, Send-time optimizer

Design guidance

  • SHOULD deliver time-critical interventions (fraud, breach attempts) immediately via push/SMS, within minutes.
  • SHOULD defer non-urgent interventions to natural review periods or break points (e.g., Monday mornings, statement arrival, year-end) rather than leisure time.
  • SHOULD defer additional notifications while the user is already actively engaged.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Notifications increase app engagement 3.5-fold in the following hour; effectiveness falls ~11% if the user opened the app the previous day; defer if the app was opened within the last hour (Ch10.2).
