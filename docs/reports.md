# Reports
{: .no_toc }

One report for each cohort and section of the book club. Each shows who attended and whether they kept coming, how people answered the poll questions, how long they stayed, and where they work. Expert Engagement is separate: it shows how outside experts invited to contribute are engaging with the curriculum.

The reports show totals only. They never show a name, an email address, or a group small enough to point to one person.
