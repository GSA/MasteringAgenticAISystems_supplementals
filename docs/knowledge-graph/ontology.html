Ontology

Nodes are non-divisible components with exactly one element kind and one layer. Relationships are asserted in canonical direction only; inverses are declared with owl:inverseOf. Domain and range are enforced by SHACL. Machine-readable files: kg/ara-ontology.ttl, kg/ara-shapes.ttl.

KindDefinitionComponents

InterfaceA point of access exposing a component's services through a defined protocol or contract (API, tool protocol endpoint).34
Infrastructure resourceA compute, accelerator, network, or execution-isolation resource on which software, models, or stores are deployed.33
Human roleA human responsibility that participates in the architecture's runtime or governance (reviewer, operator, auditor, end user).38
