Weighted Voting Decision Fusion

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:WeightedVotingDecisionFusion

A decision fusion aggregator that weights each component's recommendation by its historical accuracy on similar cases, or takes a majority vote across redundant components.

Responsibility. Combines recommendations by accuracy-weighted or majority voting.

Also known as: Majority voting, Redundant processing with voting

Variant of Decision Fusion Aggregator abstract

When to choose. Choose when component accuracies on similar cases are known and redundancy should catch errors through disagreement.
