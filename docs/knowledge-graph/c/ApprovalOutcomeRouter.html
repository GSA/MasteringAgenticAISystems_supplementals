Approval Outcome Router

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:ApprovalOutcomeRouter

A workflow routing component that, on resumption after a human decision, directs execution by that decision: approvals to execution, rejections to alternative-proposal or escalation paths, modifications to remediation steps.

Responsibility. Routes a resumed workflow along the path matching the recorded human approval decision.

Also known as: Conditional execution phase, Approval decision router, Command-based routing after interrupt

Design guidance

  • SHOULD route rejected proposals to alternative-generation or escalation paths so workflow continuity is maintained.
  • SHOULD incorporate human modifications (e.g., partial or conditional approval) before proceeding with adapted execution.
