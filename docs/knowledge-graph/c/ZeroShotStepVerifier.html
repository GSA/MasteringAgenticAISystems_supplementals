Zero-Shot Step Verifier

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:ZeroShotStepVerifier

A reasoning verifier that judges individual reasoning steps by prompting a general-purpose LLM without task-specific training, outputting binary, graded or detailed judgments.

Responsibility. Scores reasoning steps using a prompted general-purpose LLM.

Also known as: Prompted LLM verifier

Variant of Reasoning Verifier abstract

When to choose. Choose when labeled reasoning-correctness data is unavailable and moderate agreement with human judgment suffices, using a properly prompted general LLM.
