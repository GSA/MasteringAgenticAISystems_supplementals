Reflexive Safety Controller

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:ReflexiveSafetyController

A pre-programmed finite-state reactive controller that executes emergency stops or evasive manoeuvres on safety-critical sensor events without consulting planning layers.

Responsibility. Executes immediate pre-programmed safety manoeuvres on critical threats.

Also known as: Reactive emergency response layer, Layer 1 reflex controller, Collision avoidance controller

Design guidance

  • MUST take precedence over all other replanning layers for safety-critical failures.
  • SHOULD favour an immediate safe-but-suboptimal action over pausing for optimal planning.
  • SHOULD signal an in-progress safety intervention so higher layers suspend until it resolves.
