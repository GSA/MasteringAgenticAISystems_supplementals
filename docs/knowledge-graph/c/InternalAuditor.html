Internal Auditor

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:InternalAuditor

An AI auditor from an independent internal audit team who audits AI systems and the management system at planned intervals and reports findings immediately.

Responsibility. Performs recurring internal conformance audits of AI systems and controls.

Also known as: Internal audit team
