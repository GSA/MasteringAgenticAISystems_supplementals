Verifier Ensemble Aggregator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:VerifierEnsembleAggregator

A cognition component that combines judgments from diverse reasoning verifiers by voting, weighted voting or a meta-model, treating disagreement as an uncertainty signal.

Responsibility. Aggregates diverse verifier judgments into one verdict with uncertainty.

Also known as: Multi-model verification ensemble

Design guidance

  • SHOULD combine verifiers with diverse training data, architectures or strategies; homogeneous ensembles add little.
  • SHOULD escalate to more sophisticated verification or human review when verifiers disagree.
  • MAY require unanimous agreement for approval but majority for rejection in high-stakes domains.
