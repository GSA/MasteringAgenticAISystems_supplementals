Cross-Modal Reranker

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:CrossModalReranker

A reranker that scores mixed-modality candidates (text, image, audio) against the query with a multimodal model and selects the top-K regardless of modality.

Responsibility. Fuses candidates from separate modality stores into one cross-modal relevance ranking.

Also known as: Multimodal reranking model, Coordination layer
