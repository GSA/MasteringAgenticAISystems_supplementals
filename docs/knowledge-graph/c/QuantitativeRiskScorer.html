Quantitative Risk Scorer

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:QuantitativeRiskScorer

A risk scorer that multiplies an estimated probability (0-1) by an impact magnitude (0-1) and maps the product to a risk level using numeric thresholds.

Responsibility. Computes numeric risk scores and threshold-based risk levels.

Also known as: Risk score calculator

Variant of Risk Scorer abstract

When to choose. Choose when numerical evidence is needed to track trends, compare mitigation alternatives and trigger action at defined thresholds, e.g., continuous re-scoring.
