Approval Timeout Fallback Policy

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & GovernanceVariation point (abstract)arc:ApprovalTimeoutFallbackPolicy

An abstract policy setting the automatic outcome when no approver in the escalation chain responds before the final deadline.

Responsibility. Determines the terminal disposition of expired approval requests.

Also known as: Final escalation fallback

Variants

VariantWhen to choose
Auto-Approve Timeout FallbackChoose when actions are reversible and organizational risk tolerance favors velocity over oversight (aggressive).
Auto-Reject Timeout FallbackChoose when actions are irreversible or risk tolerance is low (conservative; prevents unauthorized actions).
Escalate-Up Timeout FallbackChoose when the decision must still be made promptly and higher authority is available to make it.
Post-Hoc Review Timeout FallbackChoose for time-critical decisions whose delay harms users (e.g., emergency claims or treatments) when approval queues exceed their SLA.
Retry-Queue Timeout FallbackChoose when the request remains valid later and neither declining nor escalating further is appropriate.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD choose the fallback according to action reversibility and organizational risk tolerance.
  • MUST define explicitly what the agent does when human response misses its response-time SLO, rather than leaving the agent in an ambiguous waiting state.
