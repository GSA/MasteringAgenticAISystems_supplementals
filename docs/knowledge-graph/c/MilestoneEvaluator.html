Milestone Evaluator

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:MilestoneEvaluator

A task success evaluator that decomposes a task into outcome-critical intermediate milestones and credits each one achieved, ignoring inconsequential actions.

Responsibility. Awards partial credit for achieved milestones along a task.

Also known as: Milestone-based scoring, Key-node evaluation, Critical intermediate action scoring

Variant of Task Success Evaluator abstract

When to choose. Choose for complex tasks admitting partial credit, where locating the failing intermediate step guides optimization.
