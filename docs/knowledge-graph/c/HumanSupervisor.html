Human Supervisor

Human roleHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:HumanSupervisor

A human who monitors autonomous agent execution in real time and can pause, stop, or override it, including during early-deployment calibration.

Responsibility. Monitors running agents and intervenes when needed.

Also known as: Monitor, Trader (monitoring example), Human-on-the-loop monitor, Content moderator, Human operator (autonomous vehicle ethical dilemmas), Guardian (vs. gatekeeper), Robot supervisor

Design guidance

  • MUST retain immediate intervention capability over financial agents whose behaviour deviates from expectations.
  • MUST be able to understand, intervene in and override high-risk AI decisions and be appropriately trained (Ref9.03).
  • SHOULD be able to halt new agent decisions and review critical decisions during a safety incident.
  • SHOULD limit continuous monitoring responsibility to critical periods and rotate duties, with automated escalation when high-severity alerts go unanswered.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Monitor the first 100 interactions of a newly deployed agent to spot systemic issues (Ch1.1B).
  • With 10,000 daily decisions and 98% acceptable, HOTL requires reviewing ~200 decisions, a 50x reduction versus HITL (Ch9.2).
  • Human vigilance degrades after 20-30 minutes of monotonous monitoring (Ch10.4).
