Post-Action Review Sampler

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:PostActionReviewSampler

An oversight component that lets agent actions execute immediately and routes samples of completed actions to human reviewers, catching systematic errors through periodic audits.

Responsibility. Selects executed actions for after-the-fact human review.

Also known as: Approval-after, Post-action approval, Periodic action audit

Variant of Human Validation Checkpoint abstract

When to choose. Choose for lower-stakes reversible actions where operational velocity matters; requires the ability to reverse completed actions when reviews reveal errors.
