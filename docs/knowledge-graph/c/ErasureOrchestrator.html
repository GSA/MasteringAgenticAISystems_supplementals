Erasure Orchestrator

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ErasureOrchestrator

A governance component that executes a verified erasure request across every system holding the subject's data, applying retention exceptions, marking backups beyond use, notifying processors and recording evidence.

Responsibility. Carries out the right to erasure across the organization's data ecosystem.

Also known as: Right-to-be-forgotten executor, Deletion cascade

Design guidance

  • MUST locate data across primary databases, purchase logs, service records, analytics, warehouses, backups and third-party processors.
  • MUST retain data still needed for legal compliance, legal claims, public-interest tasks or ongoing treatment, recording what was retained and why.
  • MAY mark backup data beyond use and let normal rotation overwrite it instead of deleting individual backup records.
  • SHOULD also delete derived data such as analytics and embeddings (Ref9.05).
  • MUST document request, verification, systems searched, data deleted, third parties notified and completion.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Completed within approximately one month of the request (Ch9.7); within 30 days (Ref9.05).
