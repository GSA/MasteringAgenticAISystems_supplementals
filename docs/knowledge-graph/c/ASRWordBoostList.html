ASR Word Boost List

Data artifactModel AdaptationModelsarc:ASRWordBoostList

A configuration listing domain-specific terms and boost scores that add a decoding bonus to transcript hypotheses containing those terms.

Responsibility. Biases speech decoding toward expected domain vocabulary without retraining.

Also known as: Word boosting list, Boosted vocabulary

Relationships

configures structural

  • Automatic Speech Recognizer abstract Ch7.5

Design guidance

  • SHOULD be used as the simple first customization; it cannot overcome strong acoustic mismatch and is not a substitute for acoustic adaptation in highly specialized domains.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • About 30 minutes setup; improves domain-term accuracy 5-15% when acoustics are ambiguous; example boosted_score 20.0 (Ch7.5).
