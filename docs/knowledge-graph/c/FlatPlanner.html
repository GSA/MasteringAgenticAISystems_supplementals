Flat Planner

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:FlatPlanner

A task planner that treats every action as equally fundamental and searches sequences of primitive actions, typically under STRIPS/PDDL precondition-effect semantics, without intermediate abstract tasks.

Responsibility. Generates plans as orderings of primitive actions over the full action space.

Also known as: Flat sequential planner, Classical planner, Classical state-space planner

Variant of Task Planner abstract

When to choose. Choose for simple sequential workflows (3-10 steps) or optimization-dominant problems where provably optimal solutions matter more than fast feasible plans and the action space is small.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD be preferred for simple sequential workflows with few tasks and minimal branching.
  • MAY be used for primitive-level details beneath a hierarchical backbone.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Search space is factorial in the number of primitive actions (e.g., 500 actions for a two-week trip) (Ch5.4).
