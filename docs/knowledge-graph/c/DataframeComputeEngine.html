Dataframe Compute Engine

Software componentInfrastructureInfrastructureVariation point (abstract)arc:DataframeComputeEngine

An abstract data-processing engine that executes dataframe operations such as filtering, deduplication and text normalization for ETL transformation stages.

Responsibility. Executes bulk transformation operations over document collections.

Also known as: ETL processing engine

Variants

VariantWhen to choose
CPU Dataframe EngineChoose for datasets under ~10 million documents, infrequent (monthly or rarer) processing, simple quality filters without deduplication, or limited capacity to manage GPU infrastructure.
GPU-Accelerated Dataframe EngineChoose for billions of documents where CPU pipelines take multiple days, regular full reprocessing of large corpora, fuzzy deduplication at scale, or when GPU infrastructure already exists.
