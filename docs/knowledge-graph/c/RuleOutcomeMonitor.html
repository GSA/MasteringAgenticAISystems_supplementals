Rule Outcome Monitor

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:RuleOutcomeMonitor

An observability component that compares rule-triggered decisions with later-confirmed outcomes or business metrics to track per-rule performance and flag underperforming rules.

Responsibility. Detects rule-level decision errors and performance degradation in production.

Also known as: Outcome tracker, Rule performance tracker
