Dataset Datasheet

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:DatasetDatasheet

A structured document describing a dataset's motivation, composition, collection process, preprocessing, intended and inappropriate uses, distribution terms and maintenance, used for training, validation or testing AI systems.

Responsibility. Documents the provenance, composition and appropriate use of one dataset.

Also known as: Datasheet for datasets, Data card, Dataset sheet
