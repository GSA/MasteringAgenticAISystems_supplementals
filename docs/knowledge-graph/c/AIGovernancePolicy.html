AI Governance Policy

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:AIGovernancePolicy

A leadership-approved policy artifact stating acceptable AI uses, prohibited applications, governance decision procedures, and criteria for when human oversight is required.

Responsibility. Translates organizational values into binding rules on permissible AI use.
