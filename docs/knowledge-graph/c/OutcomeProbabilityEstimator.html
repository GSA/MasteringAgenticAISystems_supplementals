Outcome Probability Estimator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:OutcomeProbabilityEstimator

A cognition component that estimates the conditional probability of each outcome state given a candidate action, from current state, models and historical outcome data.

Responsibility. Estimates P(outcome | action) distributions for candidate actions.

Also known as: Outcome model, Transition probability estimator
