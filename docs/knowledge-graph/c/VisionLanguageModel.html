Vision-Language Model

Model assetModel ServingModelsVariation point (abstract)arc:VisionLanguageModel

A generative multimodal model combining a vision encoder with a language model via cross-attention, producing captions and answers about images, including reading visible text.

Responsibility. Generates natural-language descriptions and answers grounded in image content.

Also known as: Multimodal LLM, VLM, Neva
