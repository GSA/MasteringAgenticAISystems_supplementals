Contract Basis

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ContractBasis

A lawful basis record for processing necessary to perform a contract with the individual or to take steps the individual requested before entering one.

Responsibility. Justifies processing required to deliver a contracted service.

Also known as: Contract

Variant of Lawful Basis Record abstract

When to choose. Choose when processing is genuinely necessary, not merely convenient, for contract performance (e.g., shipping address and payment for a purchase, employee bank details, treatment as healthcare service delivery).
