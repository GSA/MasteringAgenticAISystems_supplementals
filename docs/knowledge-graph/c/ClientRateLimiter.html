Client Rate Limiter

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:ClientRateLimiter

An entry-point control that caps the request rate per client or source address, throttling repeated abusive attempts such as jailbreak probing or shedding excess traffic.

Responsibility. Throttles inbound requests per client or source.

Also known as: Source-IP rate limiting, Inbound rate limit
