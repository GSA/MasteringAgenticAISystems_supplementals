Response Confidence Modulator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:ResponseConfidenceModulator

A post-generation component that adjusts response wording, adding hedges or explicit uncertainty acknowledgments, according to confidence and grounding-support levels.

Responsibility. Aligns the expressed certainty of a response with its evidential support.

Also known as: Confidence-based response modulation, Confidence indicator, Calibrated uncertainty acknowledgment, Honest limitation acknowledgment

Design guidance

  • SHOULD express ranges and data dates rather than false precision when grounding is weak.
  • SHOULD state uncertainty explicitly when genuinely uncertain while still giving a best-effort answer with caveats.
  • SHOULD prefer "I don't know" or escalation over plausible but unreliable speculation.
