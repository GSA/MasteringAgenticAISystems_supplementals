Complete Replanner

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:CompleteReplanner

A replanner that discards the current plan and regenerates a full plan from the actual current state to the goal using the standard planner.

Responsibility. Regenerates the entire plan from scratch from the current actual state.

Also known as: Replanning from scratch, Strategic deliberative planner, Layer 4 strategic replanning

Variant of Replanner abstract

When to choose. Choose when failures are global or rare (<10% of executions), unpredictable, memory is constrained, spaces are small, latency can be tolerated, or optimality matters more than continuity.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD be used for global failures or when less than ~50% of the plan remains valid.
  • MAY run asynchronously in the background, switching to the new plan only once a better alternative is ready.
  • SHOULD NOT be used where coordinating agents or users depend on plan stability, as successive replans can differ radically.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Reactive replanning from scratch costs ~3-5 s of paused computation per failure in the delivery-robot scenario (Ch5.6).
  • Strategic fleet replanning runs periodically, e.g., every 30 minutes (Ch5.6).
