Balanced Scorecard Specification

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:BalancedScorecardSpec

A specification of acceptable ranges for 4-6 complementary agent success metrics (e.g., completion, CSAT, NPS, CES, deflection) that must all be met simultaneously rather than maximising any single metric.

Responsibility. Declares the acceptable range for each complementary success metric of an agent.

Also known as: Balanced scorecard, Multi-dimensional success targets

Design guidance

  • SHOULD define acceptable ranges, not maximisation targets, for 4-6 complementary metrics spanning completion, satisfaction, efficiency, quality and safety.
  • SHOULD make trade-offs explicit (e.g., higher completion may need more latency; more escalation of ambiguous cases may lower deflection but raise satisfaction).

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Example customer-service scorecard: task completion >=90% (not maximised), CSAT >=80%, NPS >=+30, CES <=3.0, deflection rate 85-95% (Ch8.4).
