Prioritized Review Queue

Data storeHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:PrioritizedReviewQueue

A holding store of agent-flagged cases, such as pre-screened medical images or compliance alerts, ordered by likelihood of requiring intervention, from which accountable professionals review and sign off.

Responsibility. Holds flagged cases in priority order for accountable human review.

Also known as: Radiologist worklist, Alert review queue
