Decision Override Control

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:DecisionOverrideControl

An in-workflow control, placed beside the decision summary, through which a reviewer overrides an agent decision or requests human review and records a categorized reason.

Responsibility. Captures in-workflow human overrides of agent decisions with categorized reasons.

Also known as: Override button, Request Human Review button, False-positive marking control, Interactive controls

Design guidance

  • SHOULD embed override and request-review controls in the primary workflow adjacent to the decision summary, not in settings or special modes.
  • Basic override operation SHOULD NOT require technical expertise or administration privileges.
  • SHOULD log override explanations for training purposes.
