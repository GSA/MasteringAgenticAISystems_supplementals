Similarity Exemplar Selector

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:SimilarityExemplarSelector

An exemplar selector that retrieves, for each input, the demonstrations most similar to it from the demonstration pool.

Responsibility. Selects per-query demonstrations by similarity to the test input.

Also known as: Similarity-based demonstration retrieval

Variant of Exemplar Selector abstract

When to choose. Choose when accuracy gains justify per-query retrieval cost; computational cost becomes high on large demonstration pools.
