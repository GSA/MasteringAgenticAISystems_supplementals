Episode Summarizer

Software componentMemoryCognition & Memoryarc:EpisodeSummarizer

A consolidation component that prompts a language model to compress a verbose episode into an essential narrative of problem, root cause, solution, outcome metrics and lessons learned.

Responsibility. Compresses individual raw episodes into compact decision-relevant summaries.

Also known as: Episode compressor, Lossy consolidation, Session summaries

Design guidance

  • SHOULD preserve problem, root cause, solution steps, outcome metrics and lessons while discarding phrasing and routine procedural detail.
  • MAY retain original episodes temporarily before applying lossy summarization so recent cases keep full detail.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Summaries occupy 5-10% of original episode size while retaining 80-90% of decision-relevant information (Ch5.7).
  • Summarization reduces storage by 90%+ (Ch5.7).
  • Originals may be retained 30-90 days before lossy consolidation (Ch5.7).
