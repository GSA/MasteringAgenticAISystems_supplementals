Transparency Policy

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:TransparencyPolicy

An organizational policy specifying how disclosure, data governance, decision-logic, outcome-explanation and ethical-governance transparency are implemented across contexts.

Responsibility. Operationalizes transparency obligations into concrete, context-specific requirements.

Also known as: Comprehensive transparency policy, Calibrated disclosure policy

Design guidance

  • SHOULD disclose data sources, storage and retention, intentional data exclusions, training objectives, known limitations, error rates and uncertainty handling.
  • SHOULD describe discrimination safeguards, bias monitoring and harmful-output procedures specifically rather than vaguely.
  • SHOULD disclose which factors mattered and roughly how much weight they carried without exposing exact scoring formulas or thresholds that adversaries could exploit.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Example: diagnostic AI 95% accurate for common conditions but 60% for rare diseases should be disclosed (Ch9.3).
