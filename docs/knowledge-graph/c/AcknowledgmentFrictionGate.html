Acknowledgment Friction Gate

Software componentHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:AcknowledgmentFrictionGate

An oversight gate that requires the human decision-maker to explicitly acknowledge stated uncertainties before a high-stakes agent recommendation is implemented, without preventing informed acceptance.

Responsibility. Blocks reflexive acceptance of high-stakes recommendations until uncertainties are acknowledged.

Also known as: Mandatory acknowledgment, Friction mechanism
