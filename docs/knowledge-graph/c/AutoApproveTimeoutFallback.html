Auto-Approve Timeout Fallback

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:AutoApproveTimeoutFallback

A timeout fallback policy that automatically approves expired approval requests to preserve operational velocity.

Responsibility. Prioritizes velocity over oversight when approvers never respond.

Variant of Approval Timeout Fallback Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose when actions are reversible and organizational risk tolerance favors velocity over oversight (aggressive).

Design guidance

  • MAY auto-approve routine low-risk requests on timeout when delay cost exceeds the risk of an occasional suboptimal approval; high-risk decisions SHOULD escalate instead.
