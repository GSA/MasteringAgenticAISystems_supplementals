Data Retention Enforcer

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:DataRetentionEnforcer

A scheduled governance job that deletes personal data past its retention period and records an auditable deletion event for each completed deletion.

Responsibility. Automatically deletes personal data whose retention period has expired.

Also known as: Automated deletion routine, Data deletion job
