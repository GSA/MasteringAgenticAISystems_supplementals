Fairness Auditor

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:FairnessAuditor

A member of a diverse review team (affected-community representatives, domain experts, ethicists) who qualitatively reviews model outputs and flagged features for bias that metrics miss.

Responsibility. Identifies subtle, contextual, cultural and intersectional bias through expert human review.

Also known as: Qualitative auditor, Diverse review team, Fairness specialist

Design guidance

  • SHOULD have psychological safety and organizational authority to delay or modify projects over fairness issues.
