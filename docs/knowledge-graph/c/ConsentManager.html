Consent Manager

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ConsentManager

A governance service that presents granular per-purpose consent requests, records affirmative choices and processes withdrawals received through any channel so they take effect downstream.

Responsibility. Manages the lifecycle of per-purpose consent from request to withdrawal.

Also known as: Consent management system, Consent management platform

Design guidance

  • MUST request consent separately for each distinct purpose, with no pre-checked boxes, silence or implied consent.
  • MUST make withdrawal as easy as giving consent (e.g., the same single tap).
  • MUST NOT make consent for non-essential processing, such as marketing, a condition of the core service.
  • SHOULD present optional requests (e.g., marketing) after users have experienced the service's value.
  • SHOULD obtain opt-in authorization for specific proactive behaviours with explicit data scopes rather than blanket permission for any proactive purpose.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Withdrawals take effect within 24 hours regardless of channel: in person, web portal, phone or written notice (Ch9.7 hospital).
  • Withdrawing nutrition consent deletes meal photos and food logs within 24 hours (Ch9.7 wellness app).
