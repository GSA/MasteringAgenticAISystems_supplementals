Content Freshness Policy

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ContentFreshnessPolicy

A policy of staleness detection rules setting review age thresholds and check frequencies by topic sensitivity, plus expiration handling for time-sensitive content.

Responsibility. Defines when content is considered stale and must be reviewed or archived.

Also known as: Staleness detection rules, Freshness thresholds

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Technical documentation older than 6 months triggers review; regulatory content receives weekly freshness checks (Ch6.4).
  • Example thresholds: product specifications older than 90 days; policy documents unchanged for 12 months despite known updates (Ch10.1).
