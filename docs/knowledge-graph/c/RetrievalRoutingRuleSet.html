Data artifactKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:RetrievalRoutingRuleSet

A configuration of query-type-to-strategy mappings, confidence thresholds and always-retrieve patterns (e.g., product names, pricing, medical or policy topics) that governs adaptive retrieval decisions.

Responsibility. Specifies when and how adaptive retrieval must retrieve.
