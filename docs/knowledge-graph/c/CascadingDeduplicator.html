Cascading Deduplicator

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:CascadingDeduplicator

A deduplicator that runs exact, fuzzy and semantic deduplication sequentially so each more expensive level only processes survivors of the cheaper ones.

Responsibility. Sequences deduplication levels from cheapest to most expensive.

Also known as: Three-level deduplication, Multi-level deduplication pipeline

Variant of Content Deduplicator abstract

When to choose. Choose when a corpus contains exact, near and semantic duplicates together (typical in production); orders cheap levels first to minimise cost.
