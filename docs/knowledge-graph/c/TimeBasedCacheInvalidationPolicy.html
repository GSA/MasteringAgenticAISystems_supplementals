Time-Based Cache Invalidation Policy

Data artifactTools & IntegrationOrchestration & Toolsarc:TimeBasedCacheInvalidationPolicy

A cache invalidation policy that expires cached tool results after a fixed time-to-live or periodic refresh interval matched to the source's update cadence.

Responsibility. Expires cached results after a configured TTL.

Also known as: TTL invalidation

Variant of Cache Invalidation Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose when the source updates on a known schedule and no change notifications are available.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD set the TTL to the source update cadence (e.g., 24h for a daily-updated paper index, 1h for weather forecasts).

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • ArXiv cache refreshed every 24 hours; weather forecasts cached with 1-hour TTL (Ch7.3).
  • 5-minute entry validity for cached inventory results (Ch8.1).
