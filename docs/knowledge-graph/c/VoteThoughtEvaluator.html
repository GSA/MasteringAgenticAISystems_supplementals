Vote Thought Evaluator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:VoteThoughtEvaluator

A thought state evaluator that presents all candidate states at one tree level together and asks the model to judge comparatively which is most promising.

Responsibility. Selects the most promising state by comparative judgment across candidates.

Also known as: Vote prompt, Comparative judgment evaluator

Variant of Thought State Evaluator abstract

When to choose. Choose when evaluation criteria are subjective or rating scales poorly defined (e.g., narrative coherence), or when a single best candidate must be selected.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD be preferred over value estimation for subjective evaluation domains.
  • SHOULD be limited to small branching factors, since all candidates share one prompt and may exceed the context window.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.
