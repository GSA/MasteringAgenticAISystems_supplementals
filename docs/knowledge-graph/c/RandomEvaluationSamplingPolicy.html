Random Evaluation Sampling Policy

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:RandomEvaluationSamplingPolicy

An evaluation sampling policy that selects a uniform random fraction of production traces for evaluation.

Responsibility. Selects a representative random subset of production interactions for evaluation.

Also known as: 10% random sample, Representative feedback sampling

Variant of Evaluation Sampling Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose when a representative baseline-quality estimate is needed with no production latency impact.
