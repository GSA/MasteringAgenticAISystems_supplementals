Design guidance

  • SHOULD sample decorrelated mini-batches from an experience replay buffer and compute targets with a periodically synchronized frozen target network when training neural value functions.
  • SHOULD constrain policy updates to a trust region (e.g., PPO clipping) to trade speed for stability.
  • SHOULD use policy-gradient or actor-critic methods for continuous action spaces.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Grid world: 1,000 episodes to master 4x4; average reward ~-50 (episodes 1-100), ~-20 (100-300), ~+60 (300-600), >+85 (600-1000) (Ch5.12).
  • Target network syncs every 10,000 training steps (Ch5.12).
