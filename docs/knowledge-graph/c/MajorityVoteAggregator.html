Majority Vote Aggregator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:MajorityVoteAggregator

A self-consistency aggregator that selects the answer appearing most frequently across sampled reasoning paths, counting every path equally.

Responsibility. Selects the most frequent answer across equally weighted samples.

Also known as: Standard Self-Consistency voting, Unweighted majority vote

Variant of Self-Consistency Aggregator abstract

When to choose. Choose for easy problems or small sample counts (k=3-5) where vote distributions are already clear and quality-assessment overhead is not justified.

Relationships

alternative to variability

Design guidance

  • SHOULD NOT be relied on when errors are systematic or correlated across samples, since all paths then converge confidently on the wrong answer.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • GSM8K: ~58% (CoT) to 74% (k=5, T=0.7); gains of +17.9% GSM8K, +11.0% SVAMP, +3.9% ARC-challenge, +1.2% TriviaQA at k=20 (Ch5.3).
