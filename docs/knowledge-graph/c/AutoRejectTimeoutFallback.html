Auto-Reject Timeout Fallback

Data artifactGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:AutoRejectTimeoutFallback

A timeout fallback policy that automatically rejects expired approval requests and alerts operations about the approval breakdown.

Responsibility. Prevents unauthorized execution when approvers never respond.

Also known as: Automatic decline on timeout

Variant of Approval Timeout Fallback Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose when actions are irreversible or risk tolerance is low (conservative; prevents unauthorized actions).
