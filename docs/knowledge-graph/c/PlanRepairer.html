Plan Repairer

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:PlanRepairer

A replanner that identifies the invalidated segment of the current plan and searches only for a patch reconnecting to its still-valid later segments.

Responsibility. Minimally modifies the invalidated segment of an existing plan while preserving valid segments.

Also known as: Plan repair, Local plan patcher

Variant of Replanner abstract

When to choose. Choose when discrepancies are localized, adaptation must be fast, and plan stability matters (multi-robot committed trajectories, user-communicated ETAs); good-enough solutions suffice.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD be used when failures are localized, response time is tight, or other agents and users rely on plan continuity.
  • MUST fall back to complete replanning when no feasible reconnection to the original plan exists.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Repaired delivery route preserved 40-50% of planned actions and the original arrival direction (Ch5.6).
