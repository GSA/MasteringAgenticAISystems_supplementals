Document PII Redactor

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:DocumentPIIRedactor

An ETL transformation component that replaces detected PII spans in source documents with typed placeholder masks before chunking and embedding, routing uncertain detections to human review.

Responsibility. Prevents PII from entering the knowledge base.

Also known as: Ingestion-time PII redaction, PII masking, PII redaction stage

Design guidance

  • MUST redact PII during ETL before chunking and embedding, since there is no 'un-embedding' operation short of complete re-ingestion.
  • SHOULD auto-redact high-confidence (>=0.90) detections and flag medium-confidence (0.70-0.89) detections for human review.
  • SHOULD write PII detection audit logs for compliance reporting.
  • SHOULD replace PII with typed placeholders (e.g., [PERSON], [EMAIL], [PHONE]) rather than deleting it, to preserve sentence coherence for training.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Missing ingestion PII detection led to breach notification and $1.2 million in fines (Ch6.4 case study).
  • 7% of one healthcare knowledge base contained patient PII (Ch6.4 case study).
