Chunking Policy

Data artifactKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:ChunkingPolicy

A configuration fixing target chunk size, overlap, minimum chunk size and boundary-seeking order for a document chunker.

Responsibility. Parameterizes how documents are segmented into retrievable chunks.

Also known as: Chunking parameters, Chunking strategy

Relationships

configures structural

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • 512-token target, 50-token overlap, 100-token minimum chunk (Ch6.3A).
  • 128-256-token chunks improved precision but hurt recall; 1024-2048-token chunks improved context completeness but reduced precision (Ch6.3A).
