Semantic Boundary Chunker

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:SemanticBoundaryChunker

A document chunker that splits text at semantic boundaries such as section headers and paragraph breaks, targeting an average chunk size.

Responsibility. Produces semantically coherent text chunks aligned to document structure.

Also known as: Semantic chunking, Boundary-seeking chunker

Variant of Document Chunker abstract

When to choose. Choose for text documents with structure, splitting at section headers and paragraph breaks rather than arbitrary token counts.

Design guidance

  • SHOULD attach source section and page number metadata to each chunk.
  • SHOULD chunk at semantic boundaries (paragraphs, sentences) with overlap rather than arbitrary mid-word splits.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Text chunks averaging 512 tokens in the financial-report worked example (Ch2.7).
  • Worked example: 1,000 support articles chunked into 5,000 semantically coherent chunks averaging 200 words (Ch5.8).
