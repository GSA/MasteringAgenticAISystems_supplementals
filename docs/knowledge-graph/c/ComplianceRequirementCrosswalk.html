Design guidance

  • SHOULD let a single set of performance metrics and one monitoring process serve multiple frameworks' requirements.
  • MUST layer sector-specific regulatory requirements on top of general frameworks; ISO/IEC 42001 certification alone does not satisfy them.
  • SHOULD integrate AI risk into existing enterprise risk and management systems rather than creating separate structures.
