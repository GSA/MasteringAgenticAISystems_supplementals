Explanation Expansion Controller

Software componentExperienceExperience & Human OversightVariation point (abstract)arc:ExplanationExpansionController

An abstract presentation-control component that decides when collapsed explanation layers are expanded to reveal deeper reasoning, uncertainty or data detail.

Responsibility. Determines when deeper explanation layers are revealed.

Also known as: Disclosure trigger

Variants

VariantWhen to choose
On-Demand Explanation ExpanderChoose when users can be expected to seek detail themselves (low time pressure, routine or low-stakes decisions); insufficient alone because users rarely seek additional information spontaneously.
Proactive Explanation ExpanderChoose when confidence may fall below thresholds, decisions may deviate from historical patterns, or high-stakes scenarios arise, so critical detail must surface without user initiative.
