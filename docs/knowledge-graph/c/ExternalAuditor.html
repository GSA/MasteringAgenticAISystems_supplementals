External Auditor

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ExternalAuditor

An AI auditor from an outside certification or assurance body who performs very comprehensive audits and issues a formal report or certification of conformity.

Responsibility. Independently verifies value alignment and control effectiveness.

Also known as: Independent assessor, Qualified independent auditor, Certification auditor, Third-party auditor

Variant of AI Auditor abstract

When to choose. Choose when external validation is needed for customers, regulators or certification (e.g., ISO/IEC 42001), typically annually with a formal report.
