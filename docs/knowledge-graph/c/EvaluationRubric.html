Evaluation Rubric

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:EvaluationRubric

A structured scoring specification defining quality criteria (e.g., clarity, completeness, relevance, appropriateness) and their rating scales for human or model evaluators.

Responsibility. Defines consistent scoring criteria for evaluators.

Also known as: Structured rubric, Action Quality Rubric

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Criteria rated clarity 1-5, completeness 1-5, relevance 1-5, appropriateness 1-5 (Ch3.3).
  • Tool-selection rubric: intent alignment, parameter appropriateness, efficiency, reasoning soundness, each 1-5 with anchored examples for 5, 3 and 1 (Ch3.8).
  • Judge scale: 5 completely correct and comprehensive ... 0 completely wrong or no answer (Ch4.2).
