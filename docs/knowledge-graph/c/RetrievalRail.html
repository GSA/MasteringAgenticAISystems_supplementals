Retrieval Rail

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:RetrievalRail

A guardrail that filters retrieved chunks after semantic search and before LLM context injection, redacting protected fields, rejecting unapproved sources and enforcing claim-to-passage citation.

Responsibility. Prevents sensitive or untrusted retrieved content from reaching the LLM.

Also known as: Retrieval rails, RAG protection rail

Design guidance

  • SHOULD apply field-level redaction so permitted data is kept while protected fields are removed.
  • SHOULD reject documents whose provenance is not on the approved-source allowlist even if semantically similar.
  • SHOULD exclude, sanitise, or annotate retrieved content containing discriminatory language or biased framing.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Production deployments report 50-70% fewer hallucinations with citation validation (Ch7.1A).
