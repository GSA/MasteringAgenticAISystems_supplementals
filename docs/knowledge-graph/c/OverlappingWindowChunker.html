Overlapping Window Chunker

Software componentKnowledge & DataKnowledge & Dataarc:OverlappingWindowChunker

A document chunker that emits chunks sharing overlapping content with their neighbours so that context spanning boundaries remains retrievable.

Responsibility. Splits documents into overlapping chunks that preserve cross-boundary continuity.

Also known as: Overlapping chunks, Sliding-window chunking

Variant of Document Chunker abstract

When to choose. Choose when important context spans chunk or section boundaries and continuity must be preserved, accepting multiplied storage.

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Typical chunk size 500-1000 tokens with 10-20% overlap; example 800/120 (Ref7.07).
