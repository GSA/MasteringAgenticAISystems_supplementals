Persistent Volume

Infrastructure resourceInfrastructureInfrastructurearc:PersistentVolume

Durable block, network-attached or local storage provisioned to containers through a storage claim, surviving pod restarts and rescheduling.

Responsibility. Provides storage that outlives individual container instances.

Also known as: Persistent volume claim, PVC, Named Docker volume, SSD-backed volume
