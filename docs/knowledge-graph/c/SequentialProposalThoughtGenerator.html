Sequential Proposal Thought Generator

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:SequentialProposalThoughtGenerator

A thought generator that proposes candidates one after another, conditioning each new thought on the previously proposed candidates to cover distinct regions of the space.

Responsibility. Proposes successive candidate thoughts conditioned on earlier candidates for systematic coverage.

Also known as: Propose prompt, Sequential thought proposal

Variant of Thought Generator abstract

When to choose. Choose for highly constrained, structured solution spaces where candidates should collectively and systematically cover the space (e.g., arithmetic operations).
