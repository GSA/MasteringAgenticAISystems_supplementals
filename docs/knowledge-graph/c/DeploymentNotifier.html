Deployment Notifier

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:DeploymentNotifier

A component that posts pipeline milestone notifications (start, gate failure, staging, canary start, completion) with commit, version, metrics and log links to team chat channels.

Responsibility. Keeps teams informed of deployment progress without dashboard watching.

Also known as: Deployment notifications, ChatOps notifier
