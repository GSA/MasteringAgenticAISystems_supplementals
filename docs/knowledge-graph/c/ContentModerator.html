Content Moderator

Human roleHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:ContentModerator

A human reviewer who assesses ambiguous or reported content against detailed content policies and makes final approve or block decisions with justification.

Responsibility. Decides borderline moderation cases using contextual and ethical judgment.

Also known as: Human moderator, Human reviewer
