Approval SLA Policy

Data artifactHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:ApprovalSLAPolicy

A configuration of maximum acceptable human-approval times per urgency class, together with the actions taken on breach such as parallel routing to backup approvers, pool expansion or incident response.

Responsibility. Defines approval-time objectives by urgency and the responses to their violation.

Also known as: Approval SLAs, Time-bounded decision policy, Response window

Design guidance

  • MUST bound every approval with a response window so requests never wait indefinitely.
  • SHOULD treat SLA violations as incidents signalling capacity shortfalls, not as inevitable operating characteristics.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Example SLAs: routine requests within 2 hours, standard within 24 hours, complex cases within 72 hours (Ch10.4).
  • Timeout policies typically enforce 24-72 hour response windows before timeout escalation (Ch10.4).
  • Ref10.01 workflow timeouts: simple approval 5 minutes, collaborative refinement 30 minutes, expert escalation 2 hours.
