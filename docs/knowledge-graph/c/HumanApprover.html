Human Approver

Human roleHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:HumanApprover

A human reviewer who evaluates agent-proposed consequential actions and approves, modifies, rejects, or escalates them to higher authority before execution.

Responsibility. Decides on gated agent actions.

Design guidance

  • MUST be empowered to override automated decisions, not merely flag concerns.
  • SHOULD review high-stakes decisions (healthcare, employment, credit, criminal justice, education).
  • SHOULD receive sufficient information to make informed judgments so oversight is meaningful rather than rubber-stamping.
  • SHOULD NOT be assumed error-free: decision fatigue, automation bias and social engineering degrade approval quality.
  • SHOULD be rotated to prevent fatigue and supported by decision aids highlighting factors requiring attention.
  • MUST have practical capability, not just formal permission: adequate time, training, clear override protocols, and protection from adverse consequences of good-faith overrides.
  • SHOULD record an independent judgment before viewing the AI recommendation to counter automation bias.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Approval error rates typically 5-15% depending on complexity, expertise and fatigue (Ch10.4).
  • Human-reviewed maintenance recommendations achieved 30% better planning than autonomous agents alone and 60% faster decisions than manual analysis (Ch10.4).
