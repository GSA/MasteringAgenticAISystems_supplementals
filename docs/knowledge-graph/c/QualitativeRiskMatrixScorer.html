Qualitative Risk Matrix Scorer

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:QualitativeRiskMatrixScorer

A risk scorer that places each risk in a likelihood-band by impact-category matrix and reads off a LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH or CRITICAL level.

Responsibility. Rates risks by matrix lookup over categorical likelihood and impact.

Also known as: Risk matrix, Heat map

Variant of Risk Scorer abstract

When to choose. Choose when likelihood and impact can only be judged in bands and qualitative judgment about context and acceptability matters more than numerical precision.
