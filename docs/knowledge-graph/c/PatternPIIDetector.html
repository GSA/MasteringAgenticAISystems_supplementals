Pattern PII Detector

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:PatternPIIDetector

A PII detector that matches regular-expression patterns for structured identifiers such as Social Security numbers, credit card numbers, phone numbers and email addresses.

Responsibility. Detects format-structured PII.

Also known as: Pattern-based detection, Regex PII detection, PHI pattern scanner

Variant of PII Detector abstract

When to choose. Choose for structured PII with predictable formats (SSNs, credit cards, phone numbers, emails); cheapest strategy but misses names and contextual identifiers.
