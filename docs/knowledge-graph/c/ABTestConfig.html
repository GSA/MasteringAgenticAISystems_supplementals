Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Rollback if treatment TSR >5% below control, error rate >10%, or satisfaction drops >0.5 on a 5-point scale (Ch3.1A).
  • Canary rollback tolerances: success rate 3% below stable, p95 latency +2 s, or quality score -0.1; per-metric quality tolerance 3% (Ch4.2).
