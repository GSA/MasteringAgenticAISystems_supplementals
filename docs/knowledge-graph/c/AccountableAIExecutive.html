Accountable AI Executive

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:AccountableAIExecutive

A named senior executive assigned ultimate accountability for AI governance, who allocates resources, receives risk escalations, signs risk acceptances and conducts management review.

Responsibility. Holds executive accountability for AI risk and the AI management system.

Also known as: Top management, Chief Risk Officer, Chief Medical Information Officer, Executive sponsor

Design guidance

  • MUST review the AI management system at planned intervals for suitability, adequacy and effectiveness.
  • SHOULD align incentives so responsible practice, not only speed or revenue, is rewarded.
