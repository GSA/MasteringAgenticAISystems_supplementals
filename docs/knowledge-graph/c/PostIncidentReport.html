Post-Incident Report

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:PostIncidentReport

A structured record of a production incident capturing what happened, timeline, impact, root cause, detection method, resolution and prevention actions.

Responsibility. Documents an incident's cause, impact, resolution and prevention.

Also known as: Incident report, Incident retrospective, Post-incident review, Root cause analysis record, Compliance violation RCA, Lessons-learned analysis, Root cause analysis, Lessons learned

Design guidance

  • SHOULD record timeline, impact, root cause, detection, resolution and prevention for every incident.
  • MUST record impact, timeline, direct cause, root cause, systemic issues, notifications and preventive measures.
  • SHOULD be approved by the incident manager, system owner and compliance officer.
  • SHOULD convert lessons learned into preventive measures tracked to completion and shared across the organization.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Example: vector-DB unresponsiveness raised latency 2 s -> 15 s for 33 min, 8,450 requests, 2.5% error rate vs 0.1% normal, ~$150 excess cost; fixed with 5 s timeout, retry and circuit breaker (Ref8.06).
  • Post-incident review held within 1 week of resolution (Ref9.08).
  • Regulatory notification usually within 72 hours (GDPR) or 30 days (other regimes) (Ref9.08).
