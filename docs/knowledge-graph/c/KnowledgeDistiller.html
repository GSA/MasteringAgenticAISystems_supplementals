Knowledge Distiller

Software componentModel AdaptationModelsarc:KnowledgeDistiller

A training component that trains a smaller student model to match a larger teacher model's output probability distributions.

Responsibility. Produces genuinely small models that retain most of a larger model's accuracy.

Also known as: Teacher-student distillation, Fine-tuning smaller models for specialized tasks

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • 1B student reaches ~95% of a 7B teacher's accuracy vs ~85% when trained from scratch (Ch4.3).
  • 50-70% model size reduction while retaining 95-98% of teacher quality (Ref7.05).
  • Model distillation reduces memory 40-60% (Ref8.05).
