Coordination Failure Monitor

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:CoordinationFailureMonitor

A monitoring component that detects multi-agent coordination breakdowns such as duplicate effort, dropped tasks and goal divergence from structured inter-agent messages.

Responsibility. Detects multi-agent coordination failures and triggers human intervention.
