Failure Signature Catalog

Data artifactObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:FailureSignatureCatalog

A curated playbook mapping characteristic trace patterns to failure modes (tool selection, parameter generation, interpretation, state, hallucination, reasoning errors) and their root causes and fixes.

Responsibility. Records known trace signatures of failure modes to guide classification and targeted verification.

Also known as: Debugging playbook, Error pattern catalog, Logical error taxonomy, Coordination failure signatures
