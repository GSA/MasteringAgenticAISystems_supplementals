Policy Violation Responder

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:PolicyViolationResponder

An automated remediation component that, when the policy engine blocks a prohibited action, logs full context, notifies the security team and triggers review of the agent's recent activity.

Responsibility. Initiates corrective responses to blocked policy violations.

Also known as: Automated remediation workflow
