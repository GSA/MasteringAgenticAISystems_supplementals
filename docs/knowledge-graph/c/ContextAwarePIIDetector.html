Context-Aware PII Detector

Software componentSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:ContextAwarePIIDetector

A PII detector that uses document structure and keyword proximity to field labels to classify otherwise generic values, such as numbers labelled 'Patient ID', as PII.

Responsibility. Detects values that become PII through their labelling context.

Also known as: Context-aware detection, Keyword proximity detection

Variant of PII Detector abstract

When to choose. Choose for identifiers that are PII only in context (patient IDs, account numbers, internal IDs next to field labels).
