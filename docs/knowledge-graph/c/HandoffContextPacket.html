Escalation Handoff Package

Data artifactHuman OversightExperience & Human Oversightarc:HandoffContextPacket

A context-transfer bundle passed to a human agent on escalation, containing the full conversation transcript plus structured metadata on escalation reason, detected intents and attempted solutions.

Responsibility. Carries complete automated-interaction context to the human who takes over the case.

Also known as: Escalation context transfer, Handoff protocol, Shared mental model record, Escalation Handoff Package
