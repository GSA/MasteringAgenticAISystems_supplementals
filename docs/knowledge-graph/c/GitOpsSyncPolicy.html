GitOps Sync Policy

Data artifactInfrastructureInfrastructurearc:GitOpsSyncPolicy

A per-application policy setting whether the reconciler syncs and prunes automatically or waits for manual approval of each sync.

Responsibility. Determines automated versus manually approved synchronization.
