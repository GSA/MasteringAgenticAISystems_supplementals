AI Auditor

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & GovernanceVariation point (abstract)arc:AIAuditor

An abstract independent reviewer role that examines AI governance, technical, operational, safety and regulatory evidence to determine conformance and reports graded findings.

Responsibility. Determines whether AI systems and their management system conform to requirements.

Also known as: Compliance auditor, Regulatory auditor

Variants

VariantWhen to choose
External AuditorChoose when external validation is needed for customers, regulators or certification (e.g., ISO/IEC 42001), typically annually with a formal report.
Internal AuditorChoose for continuous assurance: monthly or quarterly comprehensive audits with immediate finding reporting and verification that controls operate as designed.
