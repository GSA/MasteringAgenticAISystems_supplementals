Feedback Prioritizer

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:FeedbackPrioritizer

A component that ranks feedback themes by combining mention frequency, estimated affected users from implicit signals, and severity weighting.

Responsibility. Prioritizes improvement targets by frequency, reach and severity.

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Feedback-loop organizations report 15-30% quarterly quality improvements (Ch3.2).
  • Priority score = 0.4 impact + 0.3 (10 - effort) + 0.2 frequency - 0.1 risk (Ref10.03).
