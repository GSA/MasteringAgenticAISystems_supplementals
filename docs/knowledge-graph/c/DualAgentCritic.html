Dual-Agent Critic

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:DualAgentCritic

A reflection critic implemented by a separate model or agent that critiques the producer's output, distinct from the generator.

Responsibility. Critiques another model's output as an independent reviewer.

Also known as: Critic agent, Producer-critic pattern, Reviewer agent, Quality assurance agent, Verification agent, Fact-checking agent, Second-opinion agent, Debate agent

Variant of Reflection Critic abstract

When to choose. Choose when reducing self-reinforcing bias justifies added complexity and cost; requires critic criteria aligned with downstream consumers.

Design guidance

Quantitative guidance

As stated by the sources; verify before use.

  • Individual agent hallucination rates of 3-5% can yield system-level rates below 1% with verification agents (Ch3.10).
  • Clinical decision support: critical hallucinations 2.1% to 0.4%, overall 12% to 3.8% with verification agent, evidence linking and confidence scoring (Ch3.10).
