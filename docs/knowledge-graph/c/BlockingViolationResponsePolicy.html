Blocking Violation Response Policy

Data artifactSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:BlockingViolationResponsePolicy

A violation response policy that blocks the output and returns a standardized, polite refusal (hard or softened with explanation).

Responsibility. Refuses and withholds violating outputs.

Also known as: Hard refusal, Softened refusal with explanation

Variant of Violation Response Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose for clearly prohibited categories (e.g., illegal activity) where erring on the side of safety outweighs refusing some acceptable requests.
