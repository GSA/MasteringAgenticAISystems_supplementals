Fuzzy Match Scorer

Software componentObservability & EvaluationObservability & Evaluationarc:FuzzyMatchScorer

A response scorer that marks a response correct when its string similarity to the ground-truth answer exceeds a threshold, tolerating paraphrase.

Responsibility. Scores correctness by similarity-thresholded matching against the expected answer.

Variant of Response Scorer abstract

When to choose. Choose when correct answers may be paraphrased (e.g., differently worded delivery dates) but wrong answers must still fail.
