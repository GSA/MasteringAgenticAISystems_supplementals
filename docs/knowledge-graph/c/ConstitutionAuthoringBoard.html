Constitution Authoring Board

Human roleGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ConstitutionAuthoringBoard

A cross-functional, multi-stakeholder group of ethicists, affected-community representatives, legal experts, policymakers, domain specialists and ML engineers accountable for constitutional principle content.

Responsibility. Selects, ranks and revises the principles a constitution encodes.

Also known as: Principle designers, Cross-functional alignment team, Multi-stakeholder deliberation body
