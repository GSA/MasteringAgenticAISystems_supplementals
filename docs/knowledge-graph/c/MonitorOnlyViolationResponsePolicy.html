Monitor-Only Violation Response Policy

Data artifactSafety & SecuritySafety, Security & Governancearc:MonitorOnlyViolationResponsePolicy

A violation response policy that lets a borderline action or output proceed while logging and alerting on it for later human review of whether policy refinement or agent retraining is needed.

Responsibility. Specifies monitoring-mode handling of borderline guardrail hits.

Also known as: Monitoring flag, Monitoring mode guardrail, Log-and-allow

Variant of Violation Response Policy abstract

When to choose. Choose for borderline outputs approaching policy boundaries where immediate blocking is not required; use blocking mode for inviolable legal, ethical or safety constraints.
