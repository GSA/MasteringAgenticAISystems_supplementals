Negotiated Initiative Controller

Software componentOrchestrationOrchestration & Toolsarc:NegotiatedInitiativeController

A mixed-initiative controller with no pre-assigned ownership that continuously assesses its capability, capacity and confidence and takes or yields initiative, requesting human input when confidence drops below threshold.

Responsibility. Dynamically takes or yields initiative based on self-assessed capability and confidence.

Also known as: Negotiated mixed initiative, Confidence-triggered help request

Variant of Mixed-Initiative Controller abstract

When to choose. Choose when no fixed ownership is appropriate and initiative should flow dynamically to whichever party has the relevant capability, capacity and confidence at each stage.
