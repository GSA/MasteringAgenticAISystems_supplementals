Summary Explanation View

Software componentExperienceExperience & Human Oversightarc:SummaryExplanationView

A layered explanation view presenting a brief plain-language summary of the primary decision factors plus actionable next steps or improvement paths, without technical detail.

Responsibility. Presents a plain-language decision summary with actionable guidance to non-expert stakeholders.

Also known as: Customer-facing explanation layer, Novice mode, Basic summary tier

Variant of Layered Explanation View abstract

When to choose. Choose for novice users, customers and affected parties, and for routine low-stakes decisions: plain-language summary of primary factors with actionable improvement or recourse guidance.
