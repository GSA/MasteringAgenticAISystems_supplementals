Consent Enforcement Gate

Software componentGovernance & ComplianceSafety, Security & Governancearc:ConsentEnforcementGate

A runtime gate that permits data collection or processing for a purpose only while valid consent or another lawful basis exists, blocking non-essential trackers and halting collection immediately on withdrawal.

Responsibility. Enforces purpose-level consent at the point of collection and processing.

Also known as: Tag management system, Consent-gated cookie loader, Purpose limitation enforcer

Design guidance

  • MUST activate only strictly necessary (essential) cookies or collectors before the user makes consent choices.
  • MUST stop tracking and remove analytics or marketing cookies immediately when consent is withdrawn, sending opt-out signals to advertising processors.
  • MUST NOT use data collected for one purpose (e.g., answering questions) for marketing, model training, profiling or sharing without consent.
