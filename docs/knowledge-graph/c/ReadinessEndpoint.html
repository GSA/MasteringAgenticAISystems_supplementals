Readiness Endpoint

InterfaceInfrastructureInfrastructurearc:ReadinessEndpoint

A health endpoint reporting whether a replica can serve traffic (e.g., model fully loaded); failure removes it from load-balancer rotation without restart.

Responsibility. Reports whether the replica is ready to receive traffic.

Also known as: Readiness probe endpoint, /v1/health/ready, /v1/.well-known/ready, Health check endpoint, /v1/models
