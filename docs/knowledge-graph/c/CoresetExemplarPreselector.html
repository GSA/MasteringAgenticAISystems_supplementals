Coreset Exemplar Pre-selector

Software componentCognitionCognition & Memoryarc:CoresetExemplarPreselector

An exemplar selector that pre-selects a compact core subset of highly informative demonstrations, keeping examples that are sufficient to solve tasks and discarding redundant ones.

Responsibility. Prunes the demonstration pool to an informative core subset.

Also known as: Demonstration pre-selection

Variant of Exemplar Selector abstract

When to choose. Choose when the demonstration pool is large or noisy and a compact core subset satisfying sufficiency and necessity is needed.
