Tool Idempotency Guard

Software componentTools & IntegrationOrchestration & Toolsarc:ToolIdempotencyGuard

A guard that records successful invocations of side-effecting tools and blocks re-execution of a non-idempotent tool with identical parameters.

Responsibility. Prevents accidental duplicate execution of non-idempotent tool operations.

Also known as: Idempotency verification
